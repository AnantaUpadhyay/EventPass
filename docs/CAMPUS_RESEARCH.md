# Campus event research

Checked 4 November 2026 (India). This is a manually researched directory, not a live synchronization or a university partnership. Native events are stored separately in MongoDB and published by approved accounts.

Date policy: import dates only when an institution or organizer source supports them. A historical festival page confirms the recurring series, not a new edition. Conflicting or missing dates stay null, are labelled Dates unconfirmed, and do not pass the upcoming-date filter. Prices, availability, registration status and seat counts are not inferred. Dates should be rechecked before attending.
