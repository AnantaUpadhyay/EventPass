## logo

File: `client/public/assets/eventpass-logo.png`

Use case: logo-brand. Asset: finished transparent PNG brand emblem for EventPass, a premium campus event ticket booking app. Create one bold distinctive sculptural lowercase e monogram whose negative space suggests a folded admission ticket and forward motion. A single continuous ribbon, clever silhouette, slightly rounded geometric contours. Flat luminous violet #7048e8 with a very subtle lilac edge accent, high-end independent culture brand identity, instantly legible at 32 pixels. Centered square composition, emblem fills 80 percent of canvas. No wordmark, no typography, no extra symbols, no mockup, no sheet, no background, genuinely transparent alpha. This is a production brand asset, not a generic outline icon.
